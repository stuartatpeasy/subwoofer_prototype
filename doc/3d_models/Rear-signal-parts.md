## 1. Project use

The selected sockets are [NYS367-9](../../../Hub/3d_models/electronics/NYS367-9/README.md) and [NYS367-2](../../../Hub/3d_models/electronics/NYS367-2/README.md); the polarity switch is [100DP1T1B1M1REH](../../../Hub/3d_models/electronics/100DP1T1B1M1REH/README.md). They mount in the 1.6 mm insulating FR-4 control panel, within the sockets' stated 3 mm effective-panel limit. Check washer/nut engagement, panel strength, RCA-to-plate isolation, the switch’s full lever sweep and wire bends before fixing the cut-outs. [Mechanical design](../MECHANICAL.md) and [analogue front end](../FRONT_END.md) own the assembly and circuit.
